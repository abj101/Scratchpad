Hershey occidental glyph data notice

The stroke coordinates in HersheyGlyphs.json are derived from the Hershey
occidental font data distributed by the Usenet Font Consortium and parsed in
the CC0/WTFPL-licensed python-hershey project. Letter, digit, and punctuation
IDs follow U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 91-2; the math operators
use the corresponding dedicated Hershey IDs in the Unicode mapping below. The
coordinates retain Hershey's x-right, y-down convention. Baseline and advance
metadata were added for this resource: baseline 9 as the shared Hershey baseline. Centered math glyphs
retain their source geometry around y=0, the math axis. Advance is the source
right bearing minus the source left bearing.

The underlying Hershey font data may be used for any purpose, commercial or
otherwise, with these acknowledgements distributed with the data:

Composition and placement:
  - HersheyGlyphs.json records each source charcode in `sourceIDs`. A glyph built
    from source paths records its derivation in `composition`; the `sourceID`
    field is null for these derived forms. vocab.json `hershey_sources` lists
    only complete source glyphs equivalent to a class, so it is empty for a
    composition. No other font is used as a substitute.
  - Every record carries baseline 9 and an advance equal to right minus left.
    Math operators are centered on y=0 where their source or recorded transform
    defines a common math axis; punctuation retains its Hershey placement.
  - Explicit derived shapes are: — (extended Roman Simplex hyphen); ≈ (two
    source 1246 tilde-relation paths); ≤ and ≥ (one source 1238 bar below the
    source 1241/1242 chevron); ⊆ (one source 1238 bar below source 1256); ∉
    (source 1260 membership plus source 720 slash); ∅ (centered source 515 O
    crossed by centered source 720 slash); ∀ and ⊥ (reflected source 501 A and
    source 520 T); ¬ (joined components of source 1232 plus); ↔, ⇒ and ⇔ (source
    1261/1263 arrow components and source 1238 equality bars); ∘ (centered
    source 515 O); and ^ (source 537 Greek capital Lambda legs scaled to a
    raised caret). The exact transforms and component IDs are in the JSON.

Vocabulary data:
  - vocab.json maps labels to HWRT/HASYv2 symbol IDs and aliases from the
    `hwrt-symbols.csv` and `symbols.csv` release tables. These IDs and labels
    derive from the HWRT/HASYv2 datasets, distributed under the Open Data
    Commons Open Database License (ODbL). HASYv2 source: https://zenodo.org/record/259444
